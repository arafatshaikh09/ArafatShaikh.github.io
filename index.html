About Me

I am an MSc Mechatronics and Robotics student at the University of Leeds with a Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, passionate about designing and developing intelligent electromechanical systems. My interests lie at the intersection of mechanical design, robotics, embedded systems, and control engineering, where I enjoy transforming concepts into functional prototypes.

Through academic and engineering projects, I have gained hands-on experience in robotic hardware development, CAD design, finite element analysis, actuator and sensor integration, embedded programming, and rapid prototyping. I enjoy working through the complete engineering process — from initial design and simulation to fabrication, testing, and validation.

I am particularly interested in developing reliable robotic technologies that solve real-world challenges, combining mechanical innovation with intelligent control to create practical and impactful engineering solutions.
